Styling for Summer Entertaining

Summer in Australia generally means moving your living room outdoors, long lunches that stretch into the evening, and the deck or terrace becomes the most often used space in your home.

The colour palette we gravitate towards to counteract the heat and humidity of summer is: garden greens and soft blues along with natural textures like rattan and linen, and low and warm lighting. We’ve linked some of our favourite finds below for styling your outdoor space.

A covered terrace with classic rattan furniture and soft blues and greens working in beautifully with the garden.

Start with the setting

Before the table is set, the space needs to feel comfortable. Shade comes first, whether an awning, a sail or a good umbrella, so you can sit out under the midday sun. Then comfortable seating in outdoor-rated fabric, in colours that echo the room inside. Rattan, cane and teak all withstand sun and salt, particularly on the coast. Keep the colour palette consistent and the outdoor room becomes a natural extension of your home.

Dress the table

A block-print or simple striped tablecloth makes the table look considered. Layer it with rattan placemats, a patterned plate over a plain one, and napkins in a contrasting pattern. Bamboo cutlery adds another lovely texture to the mix. Mixing is the point: nothing needs to match, it all adds to the layered effect.

Light it softly

Overhead light outdoors is harsh and flat, so keep it low and warm. String lights above, candles in hurricane lanterns, and cordless lamps on the table are the easiest way to do it. A cordless lamp needs no wiring and moves from the dining table to a side table at the end of the night. Choose a warm white, and have any hard-wired lighting installed by a licensed electrician.

The finishing layer

Planting is the last layer. Pots of rosemary and lavender work as a lovely fragrant centrepiece, and a citrus tree in a terracotta pot makes a fabulous statement nearby. Close to the coast, look for salt-tolerant plants such as westringia and lomandra. Add a jug of foliage, a bowl of lemons or limes and a basket of throws for cooler evenings.

A considered outdoor room doesn’t need to be large. It just needs shade, comfortable seating, soft light and something green!
