Upholstery: A Roundup of Some of Our Favourites

Upholstery is where a room gets its softness, and it is often where a space comes into its own. A well-shaped bedhead, a banquette perfectly fitting a wall, a chair covered in the right fabric with beautiful detail: these are the pieces that make a scheme feel considered.

This week we are letting the images do the talking. Here are some of our favourite upholstered pieces, from bedheads through to bespoke seating.

A bold stripe paired with a plain green linen side panel gives this sofa a tailored finish. Image via Pinterest.

Bedheads

A bedhead sets the tone for the whole room. Scale matters most: wider than the bed and taller than you think usually works best. After that, it comes down to shape, fabric, trim detail and how the piece works with the wall behind.

A tall, shaped bedhead in a botanical linen adds height and softness to this bedroom in our Mosman federation project.

Our favourite Christopher Farr fabric works beautifully in a calm, textural room, with the green window frame picking up the colour in the fabric. Image via Pinterest.

A simple shaped bedhead in a plain blue linen lets the wallpaper do the talking in this single bedroom. Image via Pinterest.

A scalloped linen bedhead with rickrack trim adds a lovely detail to the children’s bedroom. Image via Pinterest.

Bespoke Pieces

Custom upholstery allows the piece to fit the room, not the other way around. Seating in an alcove, a back shaped to suit a long wall, a chair fitted with a new cover in a fabric that ties the scheme together.

Seating made to fit an arched alcove is a lovely example of upholstery working with the architecture, and the generous pattern picks up the pinks and blues in the room. Image via Pinterest.

A scalloped back adds rhythm to a long wall, and a bold blue geometric keeps this banquette fresh. Image via Pinterest.

Skirted ottomans in an embroidered fabric tuck neatly under a console and add a gorgeous hit of colour. Image via Pinterest.

A fitted seat cover with a soft pink bow gives new life to a bamboo chair. Image via Pinterest.

Fabric and Detail

Fabric choice carries as much importance as the shape. We look at durability and cleanability first, then colour and texture, and finish with the details: piping, trims, studs and pleating.

Hand-finished nailhead trim and embroidered flowers elevate a simple headboard shape. Image via Pinterest.

Contrast piping in a vivid green defines the curve of this blue chair beautifully. Image via Pinterest.

A pleated skirt in a printed linen finishes the base with a tailored detail. Image via Pinterest.

A leather handle and red piping add a wonderfully considered detail to the back of this armchair. Image via Pinterest.
